About

A cartographer of ideas

I'm a software engineer and designer who treats every product like a night sky: full of bright, disconnected points that only make sense once someone traces the lines between them. My work lives at that intersection — systems thinking with a designer's hand.

Over the past decade I've shipped data platforms, design systems, and developer tools for teams of every size. I care about the quiet details: latency budgets, type safety, the exact easing curve on a hover state.

When I'm not at a keyboard, I'm usually somewhere dark with a telescope, doing the analog version of the same job — finding patterns in the noise.
