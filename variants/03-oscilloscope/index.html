CH2 //ABOUT

I build software the way an oscilloscope reads a circuit: probe the system, isolate the signal, and make what matters visible. A decade across frontend, infrastructure, and interface design taught me that clarity is an engineering discipline, not a garnish.

My work sits where engineering meets instrumentation — dashboards that behave like lab equipment, tools that surface anomalies before they become outages, and interfaces with a favorable signal-to-noise ratio.

When I’m not shipping, I’m usually restoring vintage test gear, sketching waveform typography, or tuning a synth patch until the trace on the scope looks as good as it sounds.
