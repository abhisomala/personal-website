Personal Site — 18 Design Variants

Standalone prototypes from the design concept brief. Each commits to exactly one motif and one animation mechanic. Open any card to view that variant.

01

Terminal persona

The site is a CLI shell — typed or clicked commands navigate sections, monospace only, blinking cursor.

 02

Living blueprint

Technical drafting aesthetic — projects as annotated schematics; hover reveals dimension lines and notes.

 03

Signal / oscilloscope

Animated waveform nav and dividers — scrolling shifts frequency and amplitude on a dark single-trace display.

 04

Orbit system

Canvas orbital system — you are the center node, projects are satellites; click one to open it.

 05

Paper stack

Projects as draggable documents on a desk — flick sheets aside to reveal the ones underneath.

 06

Kinetic typography only

No images — huge type is the whole interface, scaling and skewing with scroll velocity and mouse position.

 07

Command palette site

A near-blank screen with one centered search bar — all content reached via fuzzy search, VS Code Cmd+K style.

 08

Cartography

The site as a stylized atlas — projects are map regions; zooming into a region reveals its detail.

 09

CRT boot sequence

Boots like an old computer — POST screen and loading bars, then a persistent subtle scanline overlay.

 10

Data structure visualization

The site is literally a graph of experience — nodes light up in traversal order as you scroll or click next.

 11

Circuit board

PCB traces connect the sections — hovering a nav item lights up the literal circuit path to it.

 12

Raw brutalist

One accent color, huge exposed borders, deliberately unstyled — hover inverts foreground and background.

 13

Tape deck / hardware player

Cassette-deck chrome as navigation — play/skip controls scroll between sections, a tape counter tracks position.

 14

Glass depth stack

Layered glassmorphism with scroll-driven depth of field — background panels blur as the foreground focuses.

 15

Notebook / marginalia

An annotated lab notebook — handwritten margin notes, paper texture, torn-page section transitions.

 16

Constellation

A dark starfield where projects are stars — connecting lines draw themselves into constellations as you scroll.

 17

Isometric room

One illustrated isometric room — click the laptop for projects, the bookshelf for about, the phone for contact.

 18

Weather system

Background weather shifts gradually with scroll depth — clear skies to storm to sunset as you move through the page.
