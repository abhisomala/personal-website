ABOUT

I'm a software engineer and designer who treats every product like a board layout: signal first, ornament last. For the past several years I've routed frontend systems, design tooling, and the occasional soldering-iron side quest.

My happy place is the layer where engineering and design meet — schematics on one monitor, mockups on the other. I care about latency, legibility, and the kind of polish you only notice when it's missing.

Currently prototyping at a small hardware-adjacent startup. Previously: design systems, data visualization, and far too many mechanical keyboards.
