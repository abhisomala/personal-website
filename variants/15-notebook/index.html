ENTRY 03

Experiments

EXP-01 · FLAGSHIP

OpenSight

A real-time multi-agent voice assistant that replaces screen readers with active task execution for blind and low-vision users. I built it because screen readers describe a page, they don't act on it.

  • → Top 10 finalist in North America out of 2,500+ teams, Google Developer Groups Solution Challenge 2026.
  • → Cuts a multi-step web task to under 60 seconds. Screen reader baseline is 5 to 10 minutes.
  • → Validated directly with the Blind Institute of Technology and the Virginia Department for the Blind and Vision Impaired.
