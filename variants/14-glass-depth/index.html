Layer 02 · About

Working in shallow depth of field

I’m a software engineer and designer who cares about the plane between the two: the point where a system’s architecture becomes something a person can actually see and touch. Most of my work lives in that focal zone.

For the last several years I’ve shipped design systems, rendering pipelines, and product surfaces for teams that treat polish as an engineering problem — not an afterthought sprinkled on at the end.

When one thing is sharp, everything else should quietly recede. That’s my rule for interfaces, and roughly my rule for weeks: one layer in focus at a time.
